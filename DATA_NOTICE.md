# Data attribution

**Data provided by Carbon Mapper, Inc.** This independently developed educational project uses Carbon Mapper’s published plume catalogue. Its data and derivatives remain subject to the provider’s non-commercial purpose, attribution and redistribution conditions. The terms page identifies its version as January 13, 2026. [Terms of Use](https://carbonmapper.org/terms) · [API reference](https://api.carbonmapper.org/api/v1/docs) · [Product guide](https://carbonmapper.org/articles/product-guide)

Processing includes filtering published EMIT/Tanager methane observations, normalizing types, masking provider-withheld estimates, keeping quality flags, grouping locations with H3, and aggregating counts and individual observed rates. Carbon Mapper has not endorsed this project.

The release ZIP includes a worldwide full snapshot and overlapping delta in `archives/`. The small California fixture under `demo/archive/` has 42 real observations from 2025, retrieved October 30, 2026. Request manifests, source IDs and page hashes accompany both. Raw responses can contain expiring provider-issued public asset links. Synthetic records are confined to tests.

The report includes a public-domain Natural Earth outline and locally bundled Leaflet, jQuery and MarkerCluster assets. Their notices accompany the code and generated exports. [Map notices](src/methane/assets/NOTICE.txt) · [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)
